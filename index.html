How to play
  1. Steer with taps. In Arenas, Career, Daily and Workshop races you control one marble. Tap the left or right half of the screen (or press ← →) to push it. Push the way you're already rolling. Pushes use energy that refills.
  2. Arenas match you with real players racing the same arena right now. Open spots are filled by computer racers. Arenas cost an entry fee. Win to take the prize and trophies. Finish low and you lose a few trophies. More trophies open harder arenas with bigger prizes.
  3. Career levels have 3 stars: top 3, win, and win under par. Stars open new worlds.
  4. Chests drop when you win. Start the timer and come back to open them for a marble or trail you don't own yet.
  5. Lucky Race is watch-only. Bet on a marble and get paid if it finishes high.
  6. Builder & Workshop: make your own courses and publish them for everyone to race.
